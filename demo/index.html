How it works & limitations

Your audio is converted to mono in the browser and sent to the model on Hugging Face Spaces. There, silence is trimmed and volume normalised. A frozen WavLM speech model turns the audio into an embedding, and a linear classifier trained on RAVDESS + CREMA-D (115 actors) outputs probabilities for 6 emotions: neutral, happy, sad, angry, fearful and disgust.

The stress index weights those probabilities by how strongly each emotion reflects tense, negative, high-arousal speech (angry and fearful count most, calm and neutral count zero).

  • 75% accuracy is measured on actors the model never heard. A model trained on one dataset scored only 38% on another, so your microphone and room, effectively a new dataset, will lower accuracy.
  • Trained on acted North-American English, so other accents, languages and noisy mics reduce accuracy.
  • Stress is inferred, not measured. This is not a medical or HR tool.
  • The free Space sleeps when idle. The first request may take about a minute while it wakes up.
